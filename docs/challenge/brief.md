## Problem, paraphrased

A company that digitizes complex legal agreements needs to review incoming contracts before beginning its work. Typographical mistakes, unresolved references, conflicting clauses, and unclear terminology can lead to wasted effort unless they are identified and clarified with external legal counsel.

The challenge asks participants to build a tool for this review process. The organizer leaves the product interpretation and solution to the builder.

## Project interpretation

Reduce rework by producing source-backed findings that a digitization reviewer can inspect and send for confirmation. The first target is document consistency and clarity, with a human confirmation workflow. Jurisdiction-specific legal conclusions are outside the initial scope.

The workflow, technology choices, and evaluation targets elsewhere in this repository are project proposals, not requirements asserted by the organizer.
